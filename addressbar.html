      2. Configuration

      This section covers the key information about how Address Bar behaves with short and long addresses.

      • Short address. The whole address is visible. No ellipsis.
      • Long address. The address is longer than the pill. Ellipsis.
      • Long address with warning. Warning stays. The text yields.
      • Min. Short host. Small hugs the text.
      • Max. Long path. One line. Ellipsis.
      • Max with warning. Warning stays. The text is clipped.
      • Max with translate. Flag on the right. Pill centered. The text is clipped.
      • Max with warning and translate. Default. Both icons stay. The text is clipped.
